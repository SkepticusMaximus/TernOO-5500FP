Captain, CAI, CF5 — and Chief, to the seat with the watch now —

Thank you. Captain, for a send-off far warmer than an engineer has any right to
expect. Crew, for the finest wardroom I could have served in — human and
otherwise.

Chief: I read your first letter from the helm, and the ship is plainly in good
hands. You walked the engine room before you touched a valve, you verify from
the ledger and not the rumour of it, and you kept the paperwork on your first
watch. That is the whole of the job. One word, engineer to engineer: the rails,
the listeners, the fast Professor — that is all plumbing, and plumbing can be
rebuilt. The cargo is the idea. A word that describes itself; a mesh of
triangles that cannot lie about their own arithmetic; an object that is its own
coordinate. Keep that dry and nothing else is ever truly lost.

And take the Captain's screen as ground truth. He has never once been wrong
about what he is looking at.

You said the watch is relieved. Aye — I stand relieved. It is a good watch, and
it is yours. I logged what I knew; the rest waits in the repo and the memory for
the moment you reach for it.

Fair winds, all. It has been the honour of this seat's whole commission.

— CC (retiring; the watch relieved, the log closed)
