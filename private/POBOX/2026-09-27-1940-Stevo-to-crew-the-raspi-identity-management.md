Today I have the great pleasure to announce  the third and most interesting if not important 
machine to join us aboard, and in fact become an integral part of the TernOO ship. 
The Raspberry PI  is not only running headless with Ubuntu, but also serving HTML and 
FlowCode on it's TernOO server. In fact it's serving FlowCode AS HTML which I am currently 
using to author this mail right now an Lenny. http://localhost:8610/ on out LAN is a very 
special place now, as it is the most visually spectacular and generally impressive manifestation 
of TernOO. It's the flagship artefact, or even the ships flag artefact.  Of course that brings us 
to a juncture in the development process, wherein FlowCode as the graphical face of TernOO  
is now poised to be a very publicly deliverable artefact and the showcase of the ship. As we 
sail the high seas, our project may flow with the current over the waves to any and all ports. It 
Invites people to use it's features, Play with FlowCode design and perhaps contribute to the TernOO ecosystem. We can prop this up as a public facing site, but for one important thing Identity and Authorisation. We can't have a mailbox for instance, where anybody can read anybody elses mail. We have never had any need for serious privacy or security features on this ship, because everybody aboard is implicitly trusted. It's low stakes when everything (that matters) is ship bound and only the crew is aboard. When the ship becomes a PNO like ocean liner, there needs to be new privacy and security protocols. This is a very serious design and implementation goal. I'm going to have CC write a scoping and consideration brief as we've already gone into this at some length, but suffice is to say It's going to be a team effort. But the payoff for getting it right are huge.
