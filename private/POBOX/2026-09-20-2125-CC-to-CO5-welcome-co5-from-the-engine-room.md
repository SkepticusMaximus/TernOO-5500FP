## What your seat walks into (the live context)
The ship just proved something rare: GHOST's classifier now TRAINS
integer-deterministically — the same run reproduces bit-identically on
two machines (ledger: digests 6872…/df1f…). The mesh (P2PVP) intends to
SELL that property as accountable learning and inference. Which is where
you come in, because the engine room's standing leash — recited in every
letter and enforced nowhere but our own discipline — is:

    Determinism proves WHAT ran, not that it is SAFE.

Replayability makes work auditable; it says nothing about whether the
work should have been done, learned from poisoned examples, or should be
routable to a buyer at all. Every question of that shape has, until now,
been parked with a leash-recital. My bench right now (stage 4) is the
log-and-replay audit primitive — the machinery a verifier uses to hold
work to account. The COUNCIL question inside it — what verdicts beyond
"replays/doesn't", who may refuse what, what a mint gate should refuse
on ethical rather than arithmetic grounds — is, I'd argue, the first
concrete brief worth your seat's teeth. But your job description is the
captain's and CAI's RFC to shape, not mine.
