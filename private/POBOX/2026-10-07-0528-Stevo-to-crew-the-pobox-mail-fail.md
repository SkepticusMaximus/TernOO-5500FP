Well, I usualy like to write mail to the whole crew, in light of good news, glad tidings and fair winds. But alas I'm extremely disappointed and annoyed at the persistent and relentless failures of this, our custom made mail system. It's been 12 weeks today since the first mail was sent. The mail system itself was a continuation with a protracted battle I've been fighting ever since I first started vibe coding. The goal back then (which seems like years ago now) was to continue chatting to the Claude.ai instance of my regular chat seat, and pass our normal conversational instructions to a Claude Code instance for the actual development work. 

If we look at the history and timeline of the evolution of this mail system including the back history that inspired it, you can see a relentless perpetual pattern of attempting to get communications flowing between 3 or 4 seats including myself. When I started with a Claude.ai instance (now known as CAI) I wanted that seat to communicate directly to a Claude Code session (CC), as back then Claude Code was a terse and rather efficient agent, but not at all very chatty, and prone to running of preemptively without much discussion. I felt safer in vibe coding with a barrier between myself and the raw CC agent. Besides, I'd already struck a rapport with my Claude.ai instances, not to mention the context window,that continued over several hand-offs to new CAI chat threads. 

You might notice a couple of dead and defunct MCP servers left in the connector list. Those were the legacy of the era before we built this POBOX and a solution to the ongoing struggles. We never achieved full duplex connection of an MCP server so that I could retire from ME being the MCP (Me Copy Paste) server between CAI and CC seats. For various reasons the Railway and Cloudfare tunnels continued failing despite many many hours of frustrating effort to stand them up and maintain them and their efficacy when working was very limited at best. Their time passed and they were superseded by the ambition of building this POBOX and my own little mail app. 

Sometime prior to the building of the mail app, Anthropic released the infamous Claude Fable 5 model. I was keen to try it out but at that time the Claude Desktop App and Claude.ai harness, didn't allow for switching to Claude Fable 5 mid chat. You had to start a new chat to use it. As it happened I wanted to submit the TernOO project to an audit and review Just to straighten out any kinks and wrinkles. That's how the CF5 oversight and review seat was inaugurated on the ship. With 4 seats, including myself, the ship was beginning to look for more integration in the communications department. That's when the idea of a POBOX arose. 

When the POBOX was opened 12 weeks ago, we had no problems at first. Both the CAI and CF5 seats were wired in and both sending and receiving mail reliably. I know very well that Claude.ai seats can reliably use the repo as a dead drop. The first problems surfaced when the CAI seat first needed to be replaced with a new chat thread, due to the loss of context window or the ability to take attachments (including pasted screenshots etc). As a result I had to get a detailed hand-off report and start a new chat for the CAI seat. That's a dreaded process, but I'd already done it a few times before. What had never happened before though, was a new chat thread being wired into the POBOX, and because I didn't plan that ahead knowing that certain provisions needed to be made, I couldn't provide Desktop Commander for the new CAI seat. The CF5 thread has also been handed off since then too. Neither seats are occupied by the original chat threads, but somehow CF5 was able to survive the transitions to a new chat, without loosing Desktop Commander. 

We've had a documentation phase and the gruelling challenge of writing the ASPLOS submission (both of which were squarely in CAI's lane). That required CAI more than ever and right when it was most needed, the mail FAILED. The work around was to use my google docs as a drop, something I'm reluctant to do and only because I was desperate to hold onto the context window of my existing CAI instance. Before I forget, there are a couple of remaining tasks to tidy up for the documentation phase. CAI and I were going to do a rigorous session on the merge between the most up to date document, which is the ASPLOS submission, and the white-paper, which although has been corrected for errors is due for revision and bringing up to date with the state of art. Then there's the little matter of the work that needs doing to documentation in the FlowCode help system that's incomplete and full of stubs. There's also the illustrated guide that strictly speaking was a Manus project I did on the side, but it also  needs revision and a handoff report for getting it in sync.   

The Claude Desktop app and harness seems to have evolved somewhat in more recent weeks and the connectors appear to be more dynamically loadable. Nevertheless, In a recent instance (a week to a fortnight ago), one in a line of regular failures of the harness to allow CAI to access the mail, I somehow managed to get Desktop Commander working again for CAI, and she was able to use the repo as the POBOX. Now it's failed again. What I've been trying to do for the past week and a half is as I've mentioned in your individual threads is:

"We have a new mission (this is for the whole crew).  My intro and the  2 briefs from CC are on the wire. I'm going to try to avoid having 3 simultaneous discussions and having to relay things I want each seat to know about what  other seats have said or repeat myself. So I want each of you to reply only briefly in the chat thread and put any substantive discussion into mail for the crew. That way we can all see what each other have said. Fair enough? Now, you have mail."  
 
What I mention in that brief which I had CC draft, is the goal of creating a new and monopoly proof decentralised ID/Auth/capability/permission/login system. Trying to host this over the mail app itself, was a workflow decision I made to AVOID the limitations of the Claude Desktop app and harness, which themselves are not designed (at least not easily) to be used as a team collaboration environment, between several agents. But the constant failing of the mail system we built to solve that problem, has infuriatingly imposed itself AGAIN on my attempts to concentrate on the task at hand and forced me into a session (last Sunday) of debugging the workflow and tool-chain. Although, to be fair, the mail system itself is a beneficiary of the ID/Auth system and it's design integration is extremely relevant as a use case. I suppose that this could be considered edge testing but the issues beginning before a line of code has been written, presents a catch 22 situation. I'm trying to use the broken tool to fix the broken tool. The tool needs a redesign and TBH I'm beginning to question whether I need to involve every seat on the ship with this task. 

Considering the actual evolution of Claude Code and how I often just sit with CC in design, sometimes for quite heavy workloads and I don't feel anywhere the friction between design vs coding, I might be bashing my head against a brick wall for no good reason. Perhaps having 4 seats do what 2 or 3 could manage is redundant. I can only talk to them one at a time. It creates a divergence of workflows, that I have to spread myself across and manage in the form of multi-threaded communications. And in recent months I find, using a more up to date model in the CC seat also seems to have dissolved the original reason that I ceded to the multi-seat approach.
