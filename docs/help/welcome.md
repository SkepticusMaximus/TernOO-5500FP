Two things sit in front of you, and it's worth keeping them straight from the start.

**TernOO-5500FP** is the machine — a balanced-ternary, object-oriented computer architecture. Its defining idea is that **the machine word describes itself.** On an ordinary computer, a value is just bits, and some program above the hardware has to remember whether those bits mean a letter, a price, a pointer, or a colour. On the TernOO machine, the word carries its own meaning — it knows what it is. That one move puts aspects of the operating system down into the machine, and aspects of the machine up into the software, until the usual hard line between "the computer" and "the code running on it" starts to blur.

**FlowCode** is the IDE — the workshop where you build software *for* that machine. It's the window you're looking at now, with tabs across the top. FlowCode is where you develop; TernOO-5500FP is what you develop for. The programs you make in FlowCode are real, native words on the TernOO machine.

(If it helps: FlowCode is to TernOO roughly what a development environment is to any processor — except the tie is tighter here, because the machine's self-describing word means the tools and the hardware share the same vocabulary.)

## Building in FlowCode

FlowCode doesn't force one way of working. You can draw a program as a flowchart, write it as text, lay it out like a spreadsheet, or drive it from a command shell. These aren't separate tools bolted together — they're different **views of the same underlying words**. Pick the view that fits the task.

The tabs are those views:

- [[flow|Flow]] — draw a program as a visual flowchart
- [[gui|GUI]] — lay out windows, buttons, and dialogs
- [[sheet|Sheet]] — a spreadsheet whose cells can drive your program
- [[connectors|Connectors]] — wire pieces of a program together by name
- [[shell|Shell]] — a command shell with familiar idioms
- [[text|Text]] — a dual-mode text editor
- [[babble-fish|Babble-Fish]] — see your program projected into other languages
- [[academy|Academy]] — meet GHOST and teach it
- [[mesh|Mesh]] — trade compute with other machines
- [[docs|Documentation]] — where you are now

## GHOST

The TernOO machine has its own intelligence, [[ghost|GHOST]] — a small, honest AI that is, in the long run, meant to *be* the operating system: the agent that selects, directs, and orchestrates what the machine does. Today you meet GHOST in FlowCode's [[academy|Academy]] tab, where it routes tasks, refuses honestly when it isn't sure, and learns what you teach it.

## Where to start

For the big idea, read [[ternoo-words|What is a TernOO word]] — it's the machine's whole thesis in one page. To just build something, open [[flow|the Flow tab]]. To meet the machine's mind, see [[ghost|GHOST]].

Nothing here phones home. The machine is yours, and it's built to stay that way.
