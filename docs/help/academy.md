The Academy tab
section: The tabs

The Academy is where you meet [[ghost|GHOST]] — the assistant built into TernOO — and where you can teach it. It's arranged like a classroom, with a teacher, a student, and a place where lessons happen.

## The classroom

The tab has two roles. The **Professor** is a large, capable model (see [[two-mind|the Two-Mind architecture]]); **GHOST** is the small, honest student. Between them is where teaching takes place. What the Professor says appears on the board; what GHOST says appears in its book; each stays where it belongs, so you can follow the lesson as it unfolds.

The arrangement is a classroom on purpose. The Professor teaches; the student learns; and the student keeps its own honesty throughout. The Professor doesn't do GHOST's thinking for it — it teaches, and what's learned has to become GHOST's own.

## Teaching GHOST

You can correct GHOST and add to what it knows. Give it an example and tell it the right answer; teach it to handle a phrase, or teach it to *refuse* one — knowing when to decline is a lesson you can give directly. Every lesson is kept as a plain, growing record, so GHOST's whole education is open to you: not just what it knows, but the order it learned things in.

The tab gives you the tools for this — a place to edit the lessons, a **Train** button that reports back how well GHOST is doing and where it's getting confused, and a way to look directly at GHOST's trained mind after each lesson.

## The Professor, and talking backstage

When GHOST needs more than its own mind can offer, it can ask the Professor — and it checks with you first before doing so. You can also speak to either mind directly through the **Backstage Panel**, a console that slides in from the side: the Professor's side answers plainly, and GHOST's side stays honest. What's said backstage is kept apart from GHOST's lessons, so a casual conversation never quietly changes what GHOST has been taught.

## What's ready now

GHOST's tool-finding, its honesty, and the teaching loop all work today — you can train it and watch it improve. GHOST's fuller reasoning is being built. The Professor needs its larger model present on your machine; it appears when it's available and steps aside gracefully when it isn't.
