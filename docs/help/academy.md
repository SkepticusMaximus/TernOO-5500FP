The Academy tab
section: Tabs

The Academy is the classroom where you meet [[ghost|GHOST]] — the TernOO machine's own small AI — and where you can teach it. It's laid out like a lesson: a professor, a student, and a place where teaching happens.

## The classroom

The tab is split into two roles. The **Professor** is a larger, capable model (see [[two-mind|the Two-Mind architecture]]); **GHOST** is the small, honest student. Between them sits the teaching seam — the point where a lesson passes from one to the other. What the professor says appears on the board; what GHOST says appears in its book; the utterances live where they belong, so you can follow the lesson as it unfolds.

The design is deliberately a *classroom*, not a contest — the professor teaches, the student learns, and the student keeps its own honesty throughout. The professor doesn't do GHOST's thinking for it; it teaches, and the knowledge has to become GHOST's own.

## Teaching GHOST

You can correct GHOST and add to what it knows. Give it an example and a label; teach it to handle a phrase, or teach it to *refuse* one — honesty is a lesson you can give directly. Every lesson is recorded in an append-only log, so GHOST's whole education is inspectable: you can read not only what it knows, but the history of how it learned it.

The tab gives you the tools for this: a curriculum editor, a **Train** button with a report card (accuracy, where it's confused), and a **brain scan** that lets you read GHOST's trained mind as a stream of words after each lesson.

## The Professor and the Backstage Panel

When GHOST needs more than its small mind can offer, it can consult the Professor — and it *asks first*, with your consent, before delegating. You can also talk to either mind directly through the **Backstage Panel**, a slide-in console: the professor's side speaks plainly (it's your model, on your machine), and GHOST's side stays honest. Backstage conversations are kept separate from GHOST's lessons — the corridor never contaminates the curriculum — so chatting with GHOST out of class never silently changes what it has been taught.

## What's live, what's coming

GHOST's routing, its honesty gate, and the training loop are **live now** — you can teach it and watch it learn today. GHOST's full **native inference** is on the near-term roadmap. The Professor requires the larger model to be present on your machine and shows its status honestly, appearing when it's available and stepping aside gracefully when it isn't.
